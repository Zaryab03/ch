Security

Your operational data, protected by default.

Inventory counts, payroll records, and customer data are sensitive by nature. Here’s what protects them on Creatify Hub.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, so information is protected whether it's moving or stored.

Role-based access control

Every user sees only the modules and records their role permits — down to the field level where needed.

Automated daily backups

Backups run daily and are stored separately from primary systems, so recovery doesn't depend on a single point of failure.

Redundant infrastructure

Hosting is built on redundant infrastructure to minimize downtime and support the uptime commitment in your plan.

Account-level protections

Password policies and session controls are enforced by default, with stronger authentication available for admin accounts.

Audit trail on every change

Every transaction, edit, and approval is logged and exportable — useful for internal review and external audits alike.
